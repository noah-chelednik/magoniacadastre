# The Magonia Cadastre

A dated register of sources: where accounts of unusual experiences are kept, and the weather, sky and earth records that
help place and date them, each with its access and its terms of use.

This repository holds one release of the register: the website as published at magoniacadastre.org, and the data behind
it.

- **site/**: the website, exactly as served.
- **data/v1/**: every row of the register as JSON and CSV, the counts and checksums (manifest.json), and the text
  of Method and scope (method.md).
- **data/studies/**: three worked studies (Washington, D.C., July and August 1952; Rendlesham Forest, Suffolk, December
  1980; Phoenix, Arizona, 13 March 1997), each set of accounts beside the weather, sky, space weather, seismic and
  satellite records of its own place and time. Context only: no known-cause candidate is tested and no verdict is given.

**This release:** 39,661 sources, surveyed 26 November 2026: 6,424 with open licences, 31,545 whose terms for their data are
unknown, and 1,692 restricted. Unknown is an honest answer: many sources do not state terms for their data, and a
row says so rather than guess.

**Releases.** Each release is a dated snapshot and a tag of this repository (release-YYYY-MM-DD). New releases follow
every few days; RELEASES.md lists them. When a row and a source disagree, the source's own page is the authority.

**Licences.** The rows are CC0 1.0: use them for any purpose without asking. Records copied from other sources keep their
own terms (LICENSES/SOURCES.md).

**Corrections** are welcome: noahchelednik@proton.me.
