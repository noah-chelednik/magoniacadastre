Corrections and rights

How to ask for a correction or a removal, and what happens when you do.

Asking for a correction

Write to noahchelednik@proton.me with the row identifier (shown on every row), the field that is wrong, what it should say, and the source's own page that shows it. Corrections are the most useful thing a reader can send.

Asking for a link or a row to be removed

If a row links to material you hold the rights to, or describes a source in a way you believe is wrong, write with: your name and contact; the work or source concerned and the rights you hold in it; the row identifier or the page address; what you ask to be changed; and a statement that you believe in good faith that the request is right and that the details you give are accurate.

A name in a source

The register lists sources, not their contents, and holds no personal details. If a row's wording identifies you or a relative, write and the wording will be removed. What a source itself holds is in the keeping of its holder, whose contact is on the source's own page.

What happens next

  • You will have an acknowledgement within five working days.
  • The row is checked against the source's own pages. A field found wrong is corrected in the next release, normally within a week, and listed in the corrections log with its date.
  • A link to material whose rights holder asks for it to go is withdrawn in the next release while the request is checked.
  • A row that describes a public source accurately is kept, and the answer gives the source's page that shows it; removing such a row is a judgement, never a matter of course.
  • You will be told what changed. A reader who reports a correction is credited in the log as a reader, or by name if they ask.
