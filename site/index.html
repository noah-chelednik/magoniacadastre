The three studies

Three dated, well-attested sets of accounts, each set beside the weather, sky, space weather and other records of its own place and time. The joins are context only: they set the accounts among the conditions of their time, test no known-cause candidate against them, and give no verdict.

  • Washington, D.C., July and August 1952Radar returns that the air traffic controllers at Washington National Airport and Andrews Air Force Base did not match to known traffic, with accounts of lights, on several nights between 19 July and 16 August 1952. 1,002 records near Washington, D.C.; 456 world-wide indices, launches and earthquakes.The computed sky at 20 July 1952, 07:15 UTC (night of 19/20 July): Jupiter 21 degrees up in the east.
  • Rendlesham Forest, Suffolk, December 1980Accounts of lights in and beside the forest between the twin air bases of RAF Woodbridge and RAF Bentwaters, on the nights of 25 to 28 December 1980. 572 records near Rendlesham Forest, Suffolk; 577 world-wide indices, launches and earthquakes.The computed sky at 26 December 1980, 01:00 UTC (night of 25/26 December): the Moon 39 degrees up in the south-east; Saturn 6 degrees up in the east; Jupiter 6 degrees up in the east. Saturn and Jupiter about 1 degree apart.
  • Phoenix, Arizona, 13 March 1997Accounts of lights over the city and the desert to the south during the evening. 277 records near Phoenix, Arizona; 280 world-wide indices, launches and earthquakes.The computed sky at 14 March 1997, 04:30 UTC (evening of 13 March, 19:00 to 24:00 MST): Mars 33 degrees up in the east; the Moon 27 degrees up in the west.
