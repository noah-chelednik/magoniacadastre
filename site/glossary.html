Glossary

The words this site uses for what sources record, each in one plain sentence. They are chosen to say what was recorded and no more.

account
What a person recorded or told about something they experienced, as a source holds it; one account may appear in many places.
case file
A source's own file on one or more accounts; the word is the holder's, not this site's.
study
One of the worked examples on this site: a dated place with well-attested accounts, set beside the context records of that place and time.
context record
A record of the conditions at a place and time (weather, the sky, space weather, the Earth, launches, airfields) from a source the registry lists.
context only
Shown without any test against an account: the records say what was there, never what anyone experienced.
known-cause candidate
A known thing present in the records near the place and moment (a bright planet, a launch, a re-entry, an aurora, the weather) that a later test could weigh against an account; a candidate is not a cause.
verdict on an account
A finding about an account, always stated for the reference layers used and their coverage, in one of five states (accounted for, underdetermined, indeterminate, uncovered, non-evident); this site gives none.
not found in the held records
Nothing of that kind is in the records this site holds for that place and time; it says nothing beyond those records.
licence verdict
The registry's reading of a source's terms for its data: open, restricted or unknown; it is about reuse, never about an account.
reference point
The place a study's context records are measured from; distances and the computed sky are taken there.

Sources' own names and titles keep their own words.
