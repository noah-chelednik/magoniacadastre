Terms of use

The terms on which the register is offered, in plain words.

What this site is

The Magonia Cadastre is a dated register of sources: what each source is, where it is kept, what it covers, and on what terms it can be reached and used. It is free to read and to download, with no account. It holds none of the sources' content beyond the records listed on the study data page.

No warranty

The rows are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. As that dedication says, they are given as they are, with no warranty of any kind: not that they are accurate, complete or current, nor that they are fit for any purpose. The dedication also leaves to the user the clearing of any rights other persons hold in a source. The same holds for every page of this site.

Information may be out of date or wrong. When a row and a source disagree, the source's own page is the authority.

Licence readings grant nothing

A licence verdict (open, restricted or unknown) is the registry's reading of the terms a source states, made on the date the row was checked, with the terms page linked beside it. It is a dated reading, not legal advice, and it grants nothing: no right over any source's content comes from this site. The source's own terms govern; read them on the source's own page before reusing anything from it. Unknown means the terms could not be settled from the source's pages; it never means that reuse is allowed.

What is released, and on what terms

  • The rows, their fields, the schema and every value computed for this site (sky positions, counts, ranges): CC0 1.0. They may be copied, changed and used for any purpose without asking.
  • The site's own text: CC BY 4.0.
  • Records copied from other sources into the study downloads keep their own terms; each such source and the credit it asks for is named on the study data page.

A listing is not an endorsement

A source being listed says nothing for or against the source, its holder or what it holds. The register describes where records are kept; it gives no verdict on any account.

Limitation of liability

To the extent the law allows, the compiler is not liable for any loss or damage arising from the use of this site or its downloads, or from reliance on a row or a licence reading.

Links to other sites

Links to sources leave this site. The terms and the privacy notices of those sites apply there.
