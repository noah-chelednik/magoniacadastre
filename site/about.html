About

The Magonia Cadastre was compiled by Noah Chelednik, Surveyor-General of the Magonia Cadastre.

Magonia is the cloud country of a ninth-century complaint: Agobard, archbishop of Lyon, wrote of people who believed that ships sailed in from the clouds. A cadastre is the most sober record a state keeps, the parcel-by-parcel register of land. This one registers sources instead of parcels: where accounts of unusual experiences are kept, the context records that help place and date them, and on what terms each can be reached and used.

It is a dated snapshot. Sources change; when a row and a source disagree, the source's own page is the authority. A source being listed is not an endorsement of its claims.

The site is static: no cookies, no tracking, no sign-in, and nothing loaded from anywhere else.

Corrections are welcome, and every one made is listed in the corrections log. How to ask, and what happens next: Corrections and rights.

The Cadastre is free and always will be. If it is useful to you, you can support its upkeep. Gifts are not tax-deductible.
