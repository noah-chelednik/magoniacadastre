Privacy

No cookies, no analytics, no tracking: only what any web host sees.

Who runs the site

The site is run by Noah Chelednik, Surveyor-General of the Magonia Cadastre. Questions about privacy: noahchelednik@proton.me.

What the site does not do

The site sets no cookies and keeps nothing in your browser. It has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no accounts and no sign-in. It loads nothing from any other site: no fonts, images, frames or page logic from elsewhere. Search, filters and the dossier form work inside your browser; nothing typed is sent anywhere.

What the host sees

The pages are served by Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, it necessarily processes each visitor's IP address and the details of each request (the address asked for, the time, the browser's own description) to deliver and protect the site, under its own data processing terms. The dossier reads the data files for the place you choose from this site, so those requests are among them. No visitor logs or analytics are turned on for the site, and the compiler receives no list of visitors.

Mail you send

Mail sent to the contact address is read by the compiler and used only to answer it and, for a correction, to make and log the change. It is not shared or sold, and it is deleted when it is no longer needed for that.

A gift, if you choose to give one, is made on the platform you choose (Ko-fi); that platform's own privacy policy applies, and the compiler sees only what it passes on.

Your data

No personal information is sold or shared. To ask what is held about you (at most your own mail), write to the contact address.
