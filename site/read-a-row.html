How to read a row

Each row describes one source: what it is, where it is, what it covers, and on what terms it can be reached and used. A row holds none of the source's content.

The fields

id Identifier
The row's permanent identifier, sixteen hexadecimal characters. It does not change, so a link to a row stays good.
title Title
The source's own name, in its own language, with an English gloss in square brackets when it is not English.
url Address
The page that best describes the source.
tier Tier
A: a context dataset (weather, the sky, geophysics and similar). B: a core source of accounts of unusual experiences (collections of accounts, case files, investigation files). C: the long tail (archives, journals, museums, report series and lists).
kind Kind
What the source is: dataset, catalogue, archive, periodical, register or service.
topics Topic codes
One or more search codes from a fixed list, given below. They are written in the words searchers use, not as this registry's descriptions of what a source holds.
time_start From
The first year the source covers, when its pages state it.
time_end To
The last year the source covers, when its pages state it; present for a source still adding records.
places Places
Where the records come from: two-letter country codes (ISO 3166-1), global, or a short region name, when the pages state them.
language Language
The language of the source's own pages (ISO 639-1).
format Format
The forms the data come in, as the source states them.
size Size
How large the source is, as the source states it. A page name in brackets says which of the source's pages gives the figure; counted from a list means the figure counts the entries the source lists.
access_status Access
What a researcher faces: open (no account needed), registration (a free account), restricted (paid, by request, or limited to some users), ungettable (nobody can get it now; the reason says why), or unknown (the source's pages did not say).
access_method Ways in
How the source can be used: api (queries answered by a server), bulk (whole files to download), web (pages to read or search), manual (by visit, letter or request).
access_reason Reason
For an ungettable source only: offline (the source is gone), paywall, licence_refused (its terms refuse reuse), or not_collected (a well-known database of accounts listed here so a reader knows it exists, and never collected from, by policy; such a row carries its name and address and nothing else).
licence_verdict Licence verdict
open: research use is allowed without asking, though conditions such as attribution may apply (the terms page says which). restricted: the terms need permission or payment, forbid reuse, or reserve all rights. unknown: no terms covering the data were found on the pages read; an honest answer, not a gap.
licence_label Licence
A plain licence name when the source names one (CC BY 4.0, CC0, an Open Government Licence, US public domain and the like). Never a quotation of terms.
licence_evidence_url Terms page
The page that states the terms. Read it before any reuse: the source's own words are the authority.
verified_on Checked on
The date the row was last checked against the source's pages.
